# MHW Client Portal Quick Guide

## Portal Link

Use the MHW Client Portal at:

```text
https://clients.mhwlivemusic.com
```

The portal gives approved hotel contacts a simple place to review entertainment schedules, booking details, holiday coverage, schedule progress, and profile information.

## Sign In

1. Go to the portal link.
2. Enter your approved client email address.
3. Click `Send sign-in link`.
4. Open the email from MHW Client Portal.
5. Click the secure sign-in link.
6. Click `Continue to portal`.

Sign-in links expire after 30 minutes and can only be completed once. After signing in, the portal session stays active for 12 hours. If you log out, request a new sign-in link.

## Home Dashboard

The dashboard gives a quick overview of the property's entertainment planning status.

Clients can review:

- This week's entertainment.
- This month and next month's entertainment counts.
- Schedule progress for the current year.
- Holiday coverage.
- Upcoming booking highlights.

## Calendar

The Calendar page gives a full view of the property's entertainment schedule.

Clients can:

- View bookings by Month or Week.
- Switch between Calendar and List formats.
- Review bookings added during the current week.
- Open detailed booking information.
- Give artist feedback for a specific booking once the booking date has arrived.
- Download calendar events.
- Review times in the property's timezone.

## Booking Details

Booking detail pages may include:

- Date, time, and venue.
- Performer name and bio.
- Headshot or promo video, when available.
- Genre and instrumentation details.
- Social media or website links, when available.
- MHW account manager and MOD contact information.

## Artist Feedback

From a booking detail page, clients can click `Give artist feedback` to submit a performer rating and hotel feedback notes for that specific booking once the booking date has arrived.

The portal connects the feedback to the booking and signed-in client contact automatically. If feedback is already on file for a booking, the portal shows a recorded status instead of allowing another submission.

## Profile

The Profile page shows the client contact, property, and MHW account manager information connected to portal access.

## Need Help

Use the `Need help?` widget in the portal to contact MHW about schedule questions, incorrect information, access issues, or requested updates.

## Notes

- Access is managed by MHW.
- There is no public signup.
- Use the email address approved for portal access.
- If a secure link is expired or already used, request a new sign-in link from the sign-in page.
